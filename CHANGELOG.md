## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-18

The first release of Vigil: vows sealed with wax.

- **Vows** from a catalog of seventy in seven parts (body, digital, speech, money, mind, heart, spirit), or your own: abstain, a daily practice, a measure a day, or hours of the day.
- **Length** of 3, 7, 21, 40, 49 or 108 days, your own, or no end; **beginning** now, at dawn tomorrow, after the last supper, or on a chosen day.
- **Why**, in your own words and your own voice; **rigor** of a novice, a monk or a hermit; a **testament** opened only when the vow is kept; a wax **seal** set by holding it.
- **The ladder of release**: the candle of waiting, your voice, a letter, three questions, a ransom, a witness, a tithe, and sleeping on it. At any step you can still hold.
- **Lapses** become an indulgence, penance, a crack in the seal, or a burned scroll; a hermit's vow can be begun again at once.
- **Temptation**: a candle to breathe with, your reason and your voice, the strength and trigger noted; a gold bead for each one held.
- **The evening examen**, **the morning rule** with warnings on the hard days, **seasons** (Sober October, Dry January, Lent, the Nativity Fast, No-spend November), **lineage** of next steps, the **rule of three**.
- **The chronicle**: the reliquary, the map of temptations, letters, tithes, and the book of hours. A **candle widget** for the home screen. English and Russian.
